Your paperwork, searchable,searchable,organized,private, on your own server.

ZeroPaper turns scans, PDFs, photos and office files into a library you can search and ask questions about. AI fills in the details when you want it to, and documents you mark private never leave your machine.

Get started View on GitHub

Everything a paper archive needs

Drop files in, and ZeroPaper reads them, files them and makes them findable. Every detail can be edited, and every step can run without a hosted AI.

OCR that fits your setup

Read scans and photos with a vision model or Mistral Document OCR, or locally with Tesseract or Docling. Scans also get a searchable PDF.

AI-proposed details

A title, date, correspondent, document type, tags and summary for each document. Choose whether AI may create new types and tags.

Hybrid search

Postgres full-text search plus embeddings computed on your machine. Dates, correspondents, types and #tags in a search become filters.

Research

Ask questions across many documents. Answers show their progress, cite the documents they used and are saved to continue later.

Side-by-side viewer

See the PDF, the extracted text or both, with the text following the page you are on.

Review inbox

Low-confidence details, possible duplicates, blank pages and processing errors, each with its own fix.

Rules

When the text, filename or source matches, set the correspondent and type, add tags or mark the document private.

Page cleanup

Pages scanned upside down or sideways are turned upright, and blank sheets and print bleed-through are found and offered for removal.

Import anything

Upload files or whole folders with progress, or let a scanner save into a watched folder.

Tidy up

ZeroPaper suggests similar correspondents, types and tags (plurals, spelling, meaning) and merges them, moving their documents and rules.

Encrypted backups

Back up the whole library as one encrypted file, on demand or on a schedule to a folder such as a NAS share.

Any AI provider, or none

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Mistral, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, xAI, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible API, chosen per task. Or turn AI off.

Ask your archive

Research searches the library, reads the documents that matter and answers with numbered citations you can open.

  • Shows each step while it works
  • Adds up amounts and shows the sum
  • Conversations are saved to pick up later

Animated example of Research. The question “What did the hail damage cost us in the end?” is researched in five steps: two searches, reading the repair invoice and the claim settlement, and adding up the amounts. The answer: the repair at Pinecrest Auto Service came to $2,340.00, Harbor Mutual paid $2,090.00 after the $250 deductible, so the hail cost $250.00 out of pocket and the claim did not affect the safe-driver discount. It cites the repair invoice and the claim settlement letter, and notes that one private checking statement also matched but was not shown to the AI.

Quick start

ZeroPaper runs with Docker Compose on a home server, a NAS or any Linux machine, with images for amd64 and arm64.

Once it's up, open it in your browser and the setup wizard creates your account and asks how documents should be read.

Setup guide

The guide also covers configuration, HTTPS behind a proxy and deploying with Kamal.

Terminal 
git clone https://github.com/jclusso/zeropaper.git
cd zeropaper
cp .env.example .env
# Set SECRET_KEY_BASE (openssl rand -hex 64)
# and POSTGRES_PASSWORD in .env
mkdir -p ingest/private backups
docker compose pull
docker compose up -d
